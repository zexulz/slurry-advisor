    Why These Factors Matter

    Rainfall

    Heavy rain can cause runoff, reducing nutrient absorption and increasing environmental pollution.

    Wind Speed

    High winds cause uneven spreading, nutrient drift, and reduced application accuracy.

    Temperature

    Ideal temperatures (5-15°C) optimize nutrient absorption while minimizing odor and volatilization.

    Humidity

    Moderate humidity reduces evaporation and helps nutrients penetrate the soil effectively.
